The spa rooms

Three quiet rooms at the back of the building

No music you did not choose, no phone at the desk ringing through the wall. The rooms are warm, the towels are heated, and you get the room for the whole booking — not until the next person arrives.

Come fifteen minutes early if you can. There is tea, a soft chair, and nothing at all you need to do.
