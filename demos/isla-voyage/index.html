Safety

The part most tour websites leave out

You are getting on a boat in a place you do not know, with people you have never met. Here is exactly who we are.

Licensed and insured

Registered with the tourism office and the coastguard. Public liability insurance covers every guest on board. We will show you the papers.

Trained crew

Every guide holds a current first aid and water rescue certificate. There are two crew in the water at every snorkelling stop.

We cancel when we should

If the sea is rough, we do not go. You get a full refund or another date. No captain of ours has ever been asked to push it.
