What this app actually does

My Workspace is a small system for one person working for themselves. Clients, tasks, hours and invoices, all in one screen, so that Monday morning starts with a plan instead of a search through messages.

  • The timer feeds the invoice. You start a timer against a client, and those minutes are already on the next bill.
  • Late invoices are impossible to ignore. They sit clearly marked on your dashboard.
