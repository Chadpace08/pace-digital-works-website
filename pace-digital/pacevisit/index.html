01 The problem

Six to ten patients a week don't turn up.

Most clinic owners have stopped counting, because counting it makes them angry. Here is what those empty chairs are actually worth.

₱1,500+ What one missed dental appointment costs. A missed haircut costs about ₱200.

1 One saved no-show a month more than covers the ₱990 fee. That is the whole argument.

How it works in most clinics today

Patients message the clinic's Facebook page. Whoever is at reception replies, checks a paper appointment book on the counter, writes the name in a box, and messages back. If she is on break, or it is Sunday, the message sits there for hours.

Reminders are supposed to go out the day before. They go out when it is quiet. On busy days they do not go out at all — and busy days are exactly when the schedule is fullest.

None of that is anyone's fault. It is just what happens when the appointment book is made of paper.

02 What it does

Booking is the easy part. We do the rest.

Taking the booking was never the hard bit. Everything that has to happen afterwards is the hard bit, and that is the part nobody has time for.

01

Patients book themselves

You get one link. Put it on your Facebook page. Patients tap it, see your real free times, and book. No app to download, no account, no password.

02

Emails send themselves

Confirmation when they book. Reminder the day before. Thank-you afterwards. Nobody presses anything — the system does it while the clinic is closed.

03

Texts and Messenger, in one tap

The message is written for you and opens in your phone ready to send — by text or on Messenger. It comes from your clinic's own number, the one patients recognise and reply to, and it costs you nothing per message.

04

Six-month recalls

Every patient who had a cleaning gets a nudge when the next one is due. This is money you are already owed and almost nobody chases it.

05

Deposits and balances

Set a deposit rule per treatment. PaceVisit works out what is owed and shows it to the patient. You record the payment — cash, GCash, Maya or transfer — with its reference.

06

No-shows, counted

You finally see the number: no-shows this month against last month. You cannot fix what nobody is measuring.

One thing it does not do yet

It does not collect money online. PaceVisit works out the deposit you asked for and shows it to the patient, then you record the payment when it arrives — cash, GCash, Maya or bank transfer, with its reference number. Card and e-wallet collection are on the roadmap.

It is on this page because you should know before you sign up, not after.

06 Common questions

Common questions.

01

Does PaceVisit send the text messages by itself?

No, and that is on purpose. PaceVisit writes the message and opens your phone's messaging app with everything already filled in. You press send. The text then comes from your clinic's own number, the one your patients already recognise, instead of an unfamiliar sender ID. It also costs you nothing per message. Email reminders are different: those send themselves with nobody touching them.

02

Can patients pay a deposit through the booking page?

Not yet. PaceVisit works out the deposit you asked for and shows it to the patient, then you record the payment when it arrives — cash, GCash, Maya or bank transfer, with the reference number. Collecting money online is on the roadmap. We would rather tell you what it does today than promise a screen that does not exist.

03

Do my patients need to download an app or make an account?

No. They tap your booking link, pick a time and put in their name and number. That is the whole thing. No app, no password, no account. It works on any phone with a browser.

04

What happens if two patients book the same slot at the same second?

Only one of them gets it. The database itself refuses the second booking rather than the app trying to check first and hoping. The second patient sees a message saying that time has just gone, and the free times reload. Double-booking is not unlikely here, it is impossible.

05

Is my patient list private?

Yes. Every table in the database has row level security switched on and scoped to your clinic, which means the database refuses to hand another clinic your records even if the app itself had a bug. The public booking page runs on a separate connection that never carries anyone's login.

06

I am not good with computers. Can I still use it?

Yes, and I set it up for you myself — your services, your prices, your opening hours, your staff. You do not start from an empty screen. If something confuses you, message me and I will fix it or change it.
