How it works

From the first message to the last dance

Step one

Check the date

Send your date first. We only take twenty weddings a year, so this is the fastest way to know whether we can even help.

Step two

Coffee, or a video call

An hour, free, no pressure. We talk about what you actually want, and what it realistically costs in your area.

Step three

A written plan and budget

You get a real budget with real numbers, not a range. If it does not add up, we say so before you book anything.

Step four

We book everything

Venue, food, flowers, music, photographer, transport. You approve, we do the chasing and the paperwork.

Step five

The day itself

We arrive before the suppliers and leave after the last guest. If something goes wrong, you will hear about it next week, not on the day.
