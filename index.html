04 How it works together

From being found online to becoming a customer

Each part plays a role in moving people from finding your business to getting in touch. A website on its own stops at step one — which is why a website on its own rarely brings in work.

Build one part and the chain breaks. Build the whole chain and the website has a real chance of paying for itself.

08 Common questions

The questions people actually ask

Straight answers, including the one about money.

01

How much does a website cost?

A simple website starts from ₱1,000. More pages, booking forms, or software behind the site cost more than that. Tell me what you need and I send you a written price before you spend anything.

02

What does Pace Digital Works do?

Pace Digital Works is a one-person web design studio in the Philippines. It builds websites for small businesses, sets them up to be found on Google, adds enquiry and booking forms, automates repeat tasks, and builds custom business software.

03

Who runs Pace Digital Works?

Charlene "Chad" Pace runs the studio alone, and does the website, the Google visibility work and the software. Chad is an accountant and bookkeeper as well as a builder, which is why the systems include the invoicing and reporting side. There is no team — one person does the whole job.

04

Where is Pace Digital Works based?

The studio is based in the Philippines and works with clients anywhere, by email, WhatsApp and Facebook Messenger. You do not need to meet in person to start or finish a project.

05

How long does a project take?

It depends on the size of the site and how quickly you can send your photos and text. You see a working version early and tell me what to change. Nothing goes live until you are happy with it.

06

Do you only build websites, or software too?

Both. Websites are one part of the work. I also build custom business systems — bookings, dashboards, reporting — and my own SaaS products, meaning software other businesses use on a monthly plan. Pace Lodgix is one of mine.

07

What do I need to have ready?

Photos, your logo if you have one, what you sell, and where enquiries should go. Nothing technical. If you have no photos and no logo, I can use professional stock photos and make you a logo.

08

How do I start?

Send one message on WhatsApp, or one email, saying what your business does. I usually reply the same day with what I would build and what it would cost.
